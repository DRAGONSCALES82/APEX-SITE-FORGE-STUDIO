COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions? We've Got Answers.

Here's what most business owners want to know before getting started.

How much does a small business website cost?

Our custom business website projects start at $500. The final quote depends on page count, requested features, content, integrations and design scope.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Our websites are designed with responsive layouts so customers can use them on phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Can you help get my business on Google?

We offer Google Business Profile setup assistance for $299, including connecting your business website. Approval, eligibility and verification are controlled by Google, and no search position is guaranteed.

Do you build custom apps or mascots?

Yes. We can quote web-based tools, interactive experiences, brand characters, logos and creative marketing graphics based on your project.

Do I need to buy a domain or hosting?

A domain name and hosting may be needed, depending on how your website is deployed. We'll discuss options and any third-party costs before launch.
